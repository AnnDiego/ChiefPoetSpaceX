# The Mocking Moon

I lift my eyes to you,
my sometimes muse,
my always nighttime partner
but tonight
you are laughing at me.

Your sardonic neon glow,
a smirk across your pock-marked face,
reflecting the sun’s warm beam
in rays of cool
slightly mocking glee.

Why (oh why) do you mock me?
I only stare up in wonder
while sipping a Coke
or eating cheese.

I imagine sterile astronauts
walking your surface, gingerly,
putting golf balls into arid soil divots,
high-fiving
while you hold back a sneeze.
